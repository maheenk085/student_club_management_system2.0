About ClubHub 2.0

One place for every university club, event and announcement.

Our Purpose

ClubHub 2.0 is the Student Club Management System of our university. It brings students, club committees and administrators together on one simple platform, so everyone can stay connected with campus life.

What You Can Do

Students can discover clubs, request to join them, register for events and read announcements. Committee members manage their own club, members and events. Administrators look after users, clubs and system reports.

How It Works

Three simple steps to get started.

1. Discover

Browse all university clubs and see what each one offers.

2. Connect

Create an account and send a request to join the clubs you like.

3. Participate

Register for upcoming events and follow club announcements.
